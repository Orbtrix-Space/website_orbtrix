## How a post is structured

The frontmatter needs `title`, `date`, and `summary`. The `summary` shows on the
news index; the body shows on the post page.

Body copy supports **bold**, *italics*, `inline code`, links, lists, and
headings. Keep paragraphs short.

- One point per line
- Plain language
- No numbers you cannot stand behind
