Orbtrix has been selected for **NVIDIA Inception**, the programme for startups building on
accelerated computing.

The work it supports is the part of our stack that is hardest to do on a laptop: the
intelligence layer underneath autonomous mission operations. Telemetry interpretation,
anomaly detection, and processing payload data close to the sensor rather than shipping
raw frames to the ground — all of it is compute-bound, and all of it has to eventually run
under the constraints of a spacecraft rather than a datacentre.

## What it means for our roadmap

Membership gives us access to development resources and technical guidance as we build
out onboard processing and the models behind it. It does not change what DISHA does
today; it shortens the distance to what we want it to do next.

More as the work progresses.
