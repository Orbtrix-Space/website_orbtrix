Orbtrix has opened the **DISHA Early Adopter Programme** to mission teams who want to
introduce autonomy into their operations without replacing the mission architecture they
already run.

The programme starts where adoption is cheapest to justify: one operational workflow,
chosen because it is the one where an improvement is easiest to measure. DISHA is
configured around the ground systems, spacecraft interfaces and procedures a team already
uses, rather than the other way round.

## What early adopters get

- Access to selected DISHA platform modules at minimal early-stage cost
- Configuration around existing mission architecture and operational workflows
- Direct work with the Orbtrix engineering team on mission-specific requirements
- An autonomy roadmap that starts with assisted operations and widens as confidence does

In return we ask for what early adopters are uniquely able to give: structured feedback,
collaboration during deployment, and validation against real operational conditions.

## Who it is for

Mission providers, spacecraft manufacturers, satellite operators, payload companies and
system integrators — teams flying today who want to reduce operational workload before
they scale, not after.

Start small. Validate the value. Expand when ready.
