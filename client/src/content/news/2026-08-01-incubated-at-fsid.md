Orbtrix is incubated at the **Foundation for Science Innovation and Development (formerly
SID)** at the Indian Institute of Science, and works out of the Innovation Centre on the
IISc campus in Bengaluru.

For a company building autonomy software for spacecraft, proximity to that particular
research environment is the practical kind of useful: the problems we work on — intelligent
systems, cyber-physical systems, decision-making under constraint — are problems the campus
has been working on for a long time.
